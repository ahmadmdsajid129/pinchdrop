## The post (main)

> I got tired of WhatsApp-ing files to myself. So I built **PinchDrop**. 🤏
>
> You **pinch** a file on your laptop in front of the webcam… and **drop** it on your phone by opening your hand. That's it. No cable, no cloud, no "send to self".
>
> What it does:
> ✋ One gesture moves photos, folders and links from PC → phone (and back)
> 📞 Your phone's calls, WhatsApp and texts show up on your PC: reply with your keyboard, answer a call with your palm
> 👥 **Huddle**: your team's laptops in one click. Drag code, project changes or your running app onto a teammate's face. Perfect for hackathons.
> 🎨 Pill Studio, sound packs, edge lighting, Gesture Studio (next slide with a swipe 👉)
> 🔒 Everything stays on your own Wi-Fi, encrypted. Camera frames are never saved. No account.
>
> Built with Rust + Tauri on Windows, Kotlin + Jetpack Compose on Android, and on-device hand tracking.

## Screenshots to take (and in what state)

Before you start:
- **Privacy:** use the **demo phone** if you don't want your real phone's name and messages in the picture: PinchDrop → Settings → *Preview with a demo phone*. Hide real phone numbers, contact names and chats.
- **PC:** Windows display scale 100–125 %, window maximised, a clean desktop wallpaper. Take shots with **Win + Shift + S** → *Window*.
- **Phone:** take screenshots with Power + Volume down. For animations, use the phone's **screen recorder**.
- **Theme:** keep the default **Night** look (it matches the posters).
