Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The short version

PinchDrop works between your own devices on your own network. We don't run servers that see your files, messages, notifications, contacts, location or camera. We don't collect analytics and we don't show ads.

Your devices

  • Files, photos, links and text go straight from one of your devices to another over your local network (or a hotspot one of you shares), encrypted. They're never uploaded anywhere.
  • The camera is used only while you're grabbing or catching. Each frame is checked in memory for your hand and thrown away: never saved, never sent.
  • Notifications, messages, calls, contacts, photos and location from your phone are shown only on the PC you paired with, and only for the features you turn on. The PC keeps them in memory; nothing is written to its disk except the files you send.
  • Huddle connects the laptops in your team directly to each other. What you share there stays between those laptops and is wiped when you leave the huddle.

Buying Pro

Purchases are handled by Dodo Payments, our reseller (merchant of record). They process your payment and email address under their own privacy policy. When you activate a licence key, PinchDrop sends the key and your PC's name to our licence service (a Cloudflare Worker), which checks it with Dodo Payments and returns your licence. We don't store anything else.

Updates

The Windows app checks GitHub (where PinchDrop is published) for new versions. GitHub sees the request like any web request.

Crash reports

Off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, only technical details about the crash are sent.

Children

PinchDrop isn't directed at children under 13.
