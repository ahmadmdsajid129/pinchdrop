Get PinchDrop

It works in pairs: one app on your PC, one on your phone.

🤖Android phoneThe PinchDrop app (APK), Android 8 and newer↓ ▶Google PlayThe Play Store version→ ⊞Windows PCWindows 10 and 11↓
  1. Install PinchDrop on both your PC and your phone.
  2. On the PC: Devices → Pair a new phone. On the phone: Pair with PC, then scan.
  3. Same Wi-Fi, no account. Pinch on the PC, drop on the phone.
