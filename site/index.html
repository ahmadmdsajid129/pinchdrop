How it works

Three moves.
That's all.

  1. 01
    Select

    Click a file, folder, photo or link on your PC, like you always do.

  2. 02
    Pinch

    Pinch your thumb and finger in front of the webcam. It lifts off the screen with your hand.

  3. 03
    Drop

    Open your hand over your phone. It lands in the Gallery or Downloads.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. PinchDrop works between your own PC and phone on your Wi-Fi (or a hotspot). There's no account and nothing goes to a server. The internet is only used to check for updates and to activate a Pro key once.

Is the camera recording me?

No. The webcam turns on only while you're grabbing something (when you click the pill or press Ctrl + Shift + Space). Each frame is checked in memory for your hand and thrown away. Nothing is saved or sent.

Which devices does it work with?

Windows 10 and 11 PCs with any webcam, and Android phones (Android 8 and newer). Your PC and phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi, or one can share a hotspot.

Can I use it without gestures?

Yes. Everything also works with clicks: drag files onto the PinchDrop window, use Send to → PinchDrop in File Explorer, or press Send on the pill.

Does everyone in a huddle need Pro?

Yes: Huddle is a Pro feature, so each laptop needs Pro or the free 7-day trial. New installs get the trial automatically, which covers a hackathon weekend or a project sprint.

How does the licence work?

One key works on 2 PCs, with all the phones you connect to them. Moving to a new PC? Remove the key in PinchDrop → Pro and enter it on the new one.

What if I don't like Pro?

Ask for a refund within 14 days and you get your money back, no questions asked. Your files and settings stay as they are.
