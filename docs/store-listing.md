# Microsoft Store listing

- **Name:** PinchDrop
- **Category:** Productivity
- **Short description:** Pinch it on your PC, drop it on your phone.
- **Description:**

  > Move files, photos and links between your Windows PC and your Android phone with a hand gesture: pinch in front of the webcam, open your hand over the phone, and it lands there.
  >
  > • Pinch and drop with your hands, or drag and drop with the mouse
  > • Your phone's calls, messages and notifications on your PC
  > • Several phones; drag files between them
  > • Huddle: your team's laptops connected in one click: drop code, project changes or your running app onto a teammate
  > • Pill Studio themes and send animations (Pro)
  >
  > Everything stays on your own Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account. Camera frames are processed in memory and never saved or sent.
  >
  > Needs the free PinchDrop app on your Android phone.
