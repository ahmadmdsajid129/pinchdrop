# The 30-second demo video

Film it with your phone on a stand (or a friend holding it), landscape, 1080p. The laptop and the phone are both in frame, on a clean desk, in a bright room. Turn on Pill Studio's **Aurora** theme and the **Comet** pack: they look best on camera.

## Shot list
| Time | Shot | On screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Close-up: a photo selected on the laptop | **Pinch it on your PC.** |
| 3–7 s | Your hand pinches in front of the webcam; the photo lifts and follows your hand | |
| 7–11 s | You move your hand over the phone and open it; the comet streaks up, the phone shows the arrival | **Drop it on your phone.** |
| 11–15 s | The photo opens on the phone | **No cable. No cloud. No account.** |
| 15–19 s | The phone rings; the pill shows the caller; you hold your palm up; it answers | **Your phone, on your PC.** |
| 19–23 s | A WhatsApp message drops in on the pill; you reply with the keyboard | |
| 23–27 s | Quick cuts: Pill Studio themes switching, Neon → Cassette → Liquid Glass | **Make it yours.** |
| 27–30 s | Logo + "Free for Windows and Android · pinchdrop" + the website address | |

## Tips
- Record the laptop screen separately with **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), so the screen looks sharp. Then cut between the phone footage and the screen recording.
- Edit with **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11, free) or **CapCut** (free). Add the text, music from their free library, and export 1080p.
- Make a vertical 9:16 version for Reels, Shorts and Status: the same shots, cropped tight.
- Keep it silent-friendly: most people watch without sound, so the text carries the story.
